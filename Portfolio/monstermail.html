Monster Mail

3D Game for children, developed in Unity 3D, based on the Monster Project and in partnership with Saxion.

Monster Mail is a children’s game based on the globally known Monster Project. The monster project incentivizes young children to be creative by allowing them to set their imagination free and design their monster so that other people can then recreate them in their own style and show the kids the power of their creativity.

We were given this challenge by Concordia Enschede, who went to International School Twente and gathered the monster drawings for us to use to create a game, so that after 3 weeks, they could organize an event where the children could play the games and see their monsters come to life.

Thank you so much for the lovely afternoon last Wednesday! It was a great success. The children had a fantastic time, and the parents and teachers were enthusiastic as well. You really created something special - well done!

Debbie & Marlyn

Concordia Enschede

Our idea was to take the powers the kids gave to their monsters and make a game where they have to explore a map and gather all the guests by borrowing their powers to fix and find things throughout the island.

I was responsible for the creative brainstorm by creating style sheets for the environment, as well as modeling the majority of the environment assets in 3D . Another responsibility of mine was to create all of the 2D assets used. I designed a home screen, a loading screen, a credits scene, and the map, as well as all the UI elements, power icons, and dialogue boxes based on the UX created by a colleague of mine.
