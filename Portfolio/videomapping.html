Videomapping Radioheads

A short 3D animation made in Blender to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the Portuguese revolution.

Video mapping project

This immersive video mapping experience was created to commemorate the 50th anniversary of the Portuguese Revolution of April 25th. The project combines 3D animation with video mapping technology to create a stunning visual spectacle.

Developed entirely in Blender, the animation features dynamic visuals and thematic elements that celebrate the historical significance of the revolution.

Project information

  • Client Cultural Event
  • Project date 2023
