Essentials

“What objects would you put in a box if you could only live with them for the next year?”

Trailer made by Francesco Bonet

AR Immersive Experience

This semester's project was focused on the development of an AR immersive experience based on Vogue Magazine. This led us to consider how we might talk about overconsumption for people who own a lot of superfluous stuff and feel like what they have is not enough. Solving the problem of overconsumption is a very difficult challenge, but a starting point could be to make people think about it a bit more and question their habits, thus developing a speculative design project.

In this sense, our app focuses on raising awareness on the topic of overconsumption, by asking the user a question that puts them in an unlikely situation and guides them to questioning their priorities: “What objects would you put in a box if you could only live with them for the next year?”. The user will only be allowed to pack the objects that fit into the box, making them realize the value each element has in their life.

The work was divided between our team of three, with me taking part in the brainstorming and ideation phase, creating stroyboards that could better illustrate our ideas. I was also responsible for implementing the UI elements into Unity, both in our first version of the project and in the final version based on the user testing, which I also contributed in. Finnally, I helped with the programming and did the box 3D model and animation.
